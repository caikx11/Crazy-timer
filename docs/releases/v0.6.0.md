# Crazy Time v0.6.0

**English** · [Português](#português)

A minimalist shutdown / sleep / hibernate scheduler for Windows, as a [Windhawk](https://windhawk.net) mod.

> ⚠️ **This mod really shuts down, sleeps or hibernates your computer** when a schedule fires. Save your work first and try a short sleep before relying on it. Tested on Windows 11. **Windows 10 is untested.**

## Install

1. Install [Windhawk](https://windhawk.net).
2. Download `crazy-time.wh.cpp` from this release's assets.
3. In Windhawk: **Create a new mod**, select everything in the editor, delete it, and paste the contents of `crazy-time.wh.cpp` (open the file in Notepad, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C).
4. Press **Compile** (Ctrl+B), then enable the mod. A tray icon appears; click it to open the panel.
5. Upgrading from the old "Power Timer" mod? Disable it first. Schedules are kept (same data file).

Check the download with `crazy-time.wh.cpp.sha256` (`Get-FileHash crazy-time.wh.cpp` in PowerShell).

## What's in it

- Schedule by duration (1 min – 7 days), at a date and time, or repeating on weekdays.
- Several schedules; edit, pause, cancel one occurrence, postpone 10 minutes.
- Warnings at 30/15/10/5/1 minute; conflicts refused; unavailable actions explained.
- Optional per-schedule "force apps to close" (shutdown only), with a data-loss warning.
- Portuguese and English; automatic/light/dark theme; high contrast.
